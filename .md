**Diagram Explanation:**

- **Prometheus (`prometheus0_eu1`)** runs on port 9090, uses a persistent volume, and is configured via `prometheus0_eu1.yml`.
- **Thanos Sidecar (`thanos_sidecar_eu1`)** connects to Prometheus, exposes ports 19090 (HTTP) and 19190 (gRPC), and shares the config file.
- Both services are on the `prometheus_network`.
- The diagram omits commented-out services for clarity.
